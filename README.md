## About

I work at the intersection of **e-commerce operations, marketplace performance, and practical problem-solving**. My focus is keeping day-to-day operations reliable, making performance and risks visible, and improving the processes behind marketplace growth.

My experience spans Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop, inventory and replenishment, fulfillment, logistics coordination, listing optimization, promotions, reporting, and process improvement.

I bring a systems-thinking approach to operations: understand the bottleneck, use the simplest tool that solves it, validate the result, and document the process so it can be repeated.

## Areas of Focus

- **Marketplace Operations:** Amazon (US, CA, UK), Walmart, and TikTok Shop
- **Inventory & Fulfillment:** Replenishment, FBA, AWD, WFS, and FBT workflows
- **Marketplace Growth:** Listing quality, SEO, keyword research, promotions, and PPC support
- **Logistics:** Shipment coordination, forwarder and 3PL evaluation, and cost comparisons
- **Reporting & Process Improvement:** Operational dashboards, spreadsheets, SOPs, and workflow improvements
